Patricia Orenza is a Filipino-born, Singapore-raised ex-marketing girlie who somehow found her way into STEM but never quite left her creative side behind.

A Business Information Systems graduate (B.Tech, Murdoch University, Class of 2026), she’s drawn to the space where business, technology, data, and creativity meet. She likes figuring out how things work, why people do what they do, and how to make things people actually want to use (and look at). When she’s not behind a laptop, you’ll probably find her on the basketball court, competing or coaching. She’s a collector of places, ideas, and little moments, always curious about what’s next and where life might take her.

Still learning, still creating, and very much a work in progress.
